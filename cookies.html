What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store on your device. They're used to make sites work, remember your choices, or measure how a site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in much the same way, and this page covers them too.

Cookies we use

This website doesn't set any cookies, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The only thing it stores on your device is your light or dark mode choice, and only if you pick one.

  • Strictly necessary: if you use the light and dark mode switch, your browser remembers your choice in local storage, so pages look the way you picked. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings.
  • Analytics: none at present. If we start measuring how the site is used, we'll only do so with your consent, and we'll list the cookies here.
  • Marketing: we don't use marketing or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google, but no cookies are set. You can read Google's privacy policy.

Links to other sites, such as the broker portal, LinkedIn or Facebook, take you to services with their own cookie policies.

Controlling cookies

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies may stop some websites working properly. The ICO's guide to cookies explains more.
