    If you need to claim

    What happens after you call us

    No call-centre runaround and no chasing. One person owns your claim.

    1. You tell us what happened

      By phone or online. We take it from there, with no forms to chase.

    2. You get a named handler

      One person who knows your case and keeps you updated.

    3. We keep you moving

      Repairs at an approved repairer, a replacement vehicle where you're covered, and your losses pursued.

    4. Settled, then aftercare

      We see it through to settlement and check in once it's done.

    Have these to hand if you can

    • Your policy number or your broker's name
    • Date, time and place of the accident
    • The other driver's name, registration and insurer
    • Photos of the damage and the scene

    Questions about your cover

    Good to know

    Why haven't I heard of BDElite before?

    We work through insurance brokers rather than selling to the public directly, so most people first hear our name when they read their policy documents. Your broker chose us to provide the added cover on your policy and to look after you if you need to claim.

    Who do I contact about my policy or renewal?

    Your broker looks after your main policy, any changes to it and your renewal. Contact us about the BDElite cover itself or a claim, on 01204 567 500 for general questions or 01204 567 567 for motor claims.

    How do I know what I'm covered for?

    Your policy documents list the cover your broker added. If you can't find them, ask your broker, or call us with your policy number or your broker's name and we'll check for you.

    Is BDElite regulated?

    Yes. BDElite is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, firm reference number 797920.

    Someone called me about an accident I didn't report. Is that you?

    Only deal with BDElite through the numbers on this page or your policy documents. If someone calls about an accident you didn't report, hang up and call us on 01204 567 567.

    How do I make a complaint?

    We'd like the chance to put things right. Our complaints procedure explains how to raise a complaint and what happens next.
