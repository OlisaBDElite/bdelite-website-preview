      Making a claim

      I've had a motor accident. What should I do?

      Make sure everyone is safe, then call the motor claimsline on 01204 567 567. The sooner you tell us, the sooner we can arrange a repair or replacement vehicle.

      What information do I need when I call?

      Whatever you have to hand. It helps to have:

      • Your policy number, or your broker's name
      • Your vehicle registration
      • When and where it happened
      • The other driver's name, registration and insurer, if there was one
      • Any photos, dashcam footage or witness details
      • A crime reference number, if your vehicle was stolen or vandalised

      Don't worry if you don't have everything. We can start your claim and collect the rest later.

      Which number do I call?
      • Motor claims: 01204 567 567
      • Breakdown, 24/7: 01204 567 497
      • Home emergency, 24/7/365: 01204 567 499
      • Household claims: 01204 567 480
      • Tools in transit claims (handled by Davies Group): 01204 567 528
      • Personal accident claims (handled by Davies Group): 01204 567 529 or personalaccidentclaims@bdelite.co.uk
      • Lost keys with a BDElite tag: 01204 567 482

      The right number for your cover is also printed on your certificate of insurance.

      The accident wasn't my fault. Will I have to pay my excess?

      If you have motor legal expenses cover, we can try to recover your uninsured losses from the person at fault. That can include your policy excess, hire costs, loss of earnings and compensation for injury.

      Will I get a replacement vehicle?

      If the accident wasn't your fault, we can often arrange a replacement vehicle while yours is repaired, from a small car right up to a 44-tonne HGV.

      If it was your fault, or your vehicle was stolen, set on fire or vandalised, you'll need Emergency Hire Vehicle cover. That provides a replacement for up to 14 days while your motor insurer deals with the claim, if they can't provide a courtesy car.

      What if the other driver was uninsured or drove off?

      Motor legal expenses cover can help you claim through the Motor Insurers' Bureau when the driver at fault is uninsured or can't be traced.

      Someone called me about an accident I never reported. Is that you?

      Only deal with BDElite through the numbers on this website or in your policy documents. If someone calls about an accident you didn't report, hang up and call us on 01204 567 567.

      Your cover

      Why haven't I heard of BDElite before?

      We work through insurance brokers rather than selling to the public, so most people first see our name in their policy documents. Your broker chose us to provide the extra cover on your policy and to look after you if you need to claim.

      How do I know what I am covered for?

      Your policy documents list the cover your broker added. If you can't find them, ask your broker, or call us on 01204 567 500 with your policy number or your broker's name and we'll check.

      Who do I contact about my policy or renewal?

      Your broker looks after your main policy, any changes and your renewal. Contact us about the BDElite cover itself or a claim.

      What does motor legal expenses cover?

      It pays legal costs for things like:

      • Recovering your uninsured losses after an accident that wasn't your fault
      • Defending you against a motoring prosecution
      • Disputes about buying, selling or repairing your vehicle
      • Your vehicle being cloned, or illegally clamped or towed
      • Unenforceable parking fines and Motor Insurance Database errors
      • Protecting your driving licence

      Exactly what's included depends on your policy, so check your documents.

      What does "reasonable prospects of success" mean?

      Legal expenses policies pay for a legal case when it's more likely than not to succeed, which means a better than 50% chance. We'll assess this for you when you claim and explain the outcome.

      Does household legal expenses cover my family?

      Household and family legal expenses covers you, your partner and any family member who lives with you permanently. It helps with things like personal injury, employment disputes, problems with goods or services you've bought, and disputes about your home.

      Where am I covered?

      Most of our cover applies in the UK, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man. Some policies go further: for example, motor legal expenses can recover losses after a non-fault accident in the EU. Your policy documents give the details.

      Can I cancel?

      Many of our products have a 14-day cooling-off period with a full refund if you haven't claimed. Your policy documents confirm this, and the broker who arranged your policy can cancel it for you.

      Breakdown and home

      I've broken down. What do I do?

      Get yourself and your passengers somewhere safe, then call 01204 567 497. The line is open 24 hours a day. Have your vehicle registration and location ready.

      Which vehicles does breakdown cover?

      Our personal breakdown cover is for cars, vans and taxis up to 3.5 tonnes. Fleet breakdown covers vehicles up to 44 tonnes, including HGVs. Vehicles need to be roadworthy, with a valid MOT and serviced to the manufacturer's recommendations.

      Can I call you out straight after buying the policy?

      New breakdown policies don't cover call-outs in the first 24 hours, unless it's a renewal or you're moving from another provider.

      What counts as a home emergency?

      A sudden event that needs fixing straight away to make your home safe, secure or liveable. For example, a burst pipe, a blocked drain, a loss of power, gas or water, broken locks, lost keys, a pest infestation or roof damage. Your certificate lists the sections of cover you have.

      Call 01204 567 499, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

      What is excess protection?

      If you make a successful claim on your home or landlord buildings or contents insurance, excess protection pays back the excess you had to pay, up to the limit on your certificate. Tell us within 31 days of your claim being settled.

      Key tags

      What is a BDElite key tag?

      A key fob with a unique code. If you lose your keys and someone hands them in, we contact you and post them back by Royal Mail Special Delivery. You need to register your tag first.

      How long do you keep lost keys?

      We hold returned keys securely for 12 months. Call the Key Recovery team on 01204 567 482, Monday to Friday 8.30am to 5pm, to check whether yours have been handed in.

      For brokers

      Do you sell direct to the public?

      No. Our products are only available through insurance brokers.

      Which broking platforms do you work with?

      Our products quote and bind by EDI in Acturis, Open GI and SSP, so there's no double keying.

      Who underwrites your products?

      Legal expenses and breakdown products are underwritten by Financial & Legal Insurance Company Limited. Home emergency is underwritten by AmTrust Specialty and administered by Arc Legal Assistance. Personal accident and tools in transit cover are underwritten by Collinson Insurance, with claims handled by Davies Group. Each policy wording confirms its insurer.

      How do I become a BDElite partner?

      Call our sales team on 01204 567 500 or email bdelite.admin@bdelite.co.uk. Existing partners can use the broker portal.

      For fleets

      How do our drivers report an incident?

      By phone, on the web or through our mobile app, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We handle every type of claim, including fault, non-fault, theft, vandalism, fire and windscreen.

      What sizes of vehicle can you cover?

      From cars and vans to 44-tonne HGVs, for breakdown, repairs and replacement vehicles. Electric vehicles are covered too, with specialist EV repairers and replacement EVs available.

      How quickly are repairs and total losses handled?

      Repairs are allocated on the first call and authorised within 24 hours, and repairs through our approved network are guaranteed for 3 years. A total loss can be decided within 24 hours from photos, with an engineer's report within 48 hours.

      Our eight-week letter or final response letter will include details of the Financial Ombudsman Service.

      If at any stage you're unhappy with how we're handling your complaint, write to the Managing Director at the address above, giving full details.

      What cookies are

      Cookies are small text files a website can store on your device. They're used to make sites work, remember your choices, or measure how a site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in much the same way, and this page covers them too.

      Cookies we use

      This website doesn't set any cookies, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The only thing it stores on your device is your light or dark mode choice, and only if you pick one.

      • Strictly necessary: if you use the light and dark mode switch, your browser remembers your choice in local storage, so pages look the way you picked. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings.
      • Analytics: none at present. If we start measuring how the site is used, we'll only do so with your consent, and we'll list the cookies here.
      • Marketing: we don't use marketing or advertising cookies.

      Third-party services

      Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google, but no cookies are set. You can read Google's privacy policy.

      Links to other sites, such as the broker portal, LinkedIn or Facebook, take you to services with their own cookie policies.

      Controlling cookies

      Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies may stop some websites working properly. The ICO's guide to cookies explains more.
